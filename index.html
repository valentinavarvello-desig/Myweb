Autoclásica

Cars, motorcycles and automotive history

About the Event

Autoclásica is an annual exhibition dedicated to classic and collector vehicles. It brings together historic cars, motorcycles, racing vehicles and automotive clubs in San Isidro, Buenos Aires.

The exhibition allows visitors to explore vehicles from different periods and countries while learning about automotive history, design and motorsport..

The event takes place from October 9th to 12th, from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM each day. During these four days, visitors can explore the exhibitions, special displays and automotive activities.

What to Expect

Classic Cars

Explore a wide range of vintage and collector cars from different eras and manufacturers.

Motorcycles

Discover motorcycles ranging from historic models to competition machines.

Racing Vehicles

See vehicles connected to Argentina's rich history of motorsport and international racing.

Automotive Clubs

Meet automotive clubs and enthusiasts who share their passion for classic vehicles.

Classic Car Competition

Watch or participate in the classic car competition showcasing the finest vehicles.

Special Exhibitions

Enjoy special exhibitions highlighting specific themes, brands or automotive eras.

Activities

A day at Autoclásica is not only about looking at cars. There are different ways to explore the event and make the most of the visit. Visitors can enjoy the following activities:

  • Walk through the classic and collector car exhibitions
  • Take photographs of your favorite cars
  • Discover historic motorcycles and racing vehicles
  • Look for rare or unusual models
  • Visit the stands of automotive clubs.

What you can find at the event

  • Food trucks with different food and drink options
  • Restrooms available throughout the exhibition
  • Areas to sit and take a break
  • Spaces to buy souvenirs and automotive-related products

Important Information

Things visitors should bring.

  • Your event ticket
  • Comfortable shoes for walking
  • Camera or smartphone for photos
  • Cash or a credit card for food and drinks

Recommendations

We recommend arriving early to have enough time to explore the entire exhibition. Since there are many cars, motorcycles and activities to see, plan to spend several hours at the event. Take your time walking through the different areas and check the event schedule so you do not miss any special activities.

Spotify Playlist

The soundtrack of F1: The Movie accompanies the atmosphere of Autoclásica with music connected to speed, racing and motorsport. It is a selection to listen to while exploring the cars and discovering the history behind them.

How to Get There

Autoclásica takes place at the Hipódromo de San Isidro, in San Isidro, Buenos Aires.
