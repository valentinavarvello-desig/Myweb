AutoClásica

AutoClásica is an annual classic vehicle exhibition held at the Hipódromo de San Isidro in Buenos Aires, Argentina.

The exhibition

Historic cars and motorcycles, themed exhibitions and a concours of elegance bring classic motoring to life.

The place

Hipódromo de San Isidro, Buenos Aires, Argentina.

The organizer

Club de Automóviles Clásicos de la República Argentina.
